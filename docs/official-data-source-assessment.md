# Official Data-Source Assessment

**Review date:** 2026-11-10 (Asia/Kolkata)

**Scope:** NSE, BSE and Yahoo Finance as a potential secondary reference.

**Status:** desk research only. No data was downloaded, no provider was activated, and no license was obtained or interpreted as permission to use a feed.

## Executive assessment

HAMSA should treat the exchanges and issuer filings as the authoritative record for Indian listing identity, venue-specific EOD prices, trading volume, delivery data, corporate actions and disclosures. Both exchanges publish official EOD/reference-data products. NSE documents downloadable daily reports and historical archives; its Data Sharing & Usage Policy also describes subscriber agreements and separately priced commercial access. BSE lists daily EOD, historical trading and corporate data products and operates a subscription-based Self Data Feed/API channel. Access to a public page or downloadable file is not proof that HAMSA may automate collection, retain a local archive, derive a commercial product or redistribute the data.

No source reviewed establishes the earliest complete date for all required equity series, historical security-master snapshots, exact post-close publication SLAs, or a blanket right to locally archive either exchange's files. Those points require product-level specifications and written license review before ingestion.

Yahoo Finance may be useful for manual, secondary historical comparison, subject to its terms. Yahoo Help documents historical quote pages/downloads for most quotes (CSV downloading is listed as a Yahoo Finance Gold feature and may be unavailable for some instruments due to licensing), and documents split/dividend adjusted-close calculations. This is not proof of complete Indian-equity coverage, exchange-grade volume, a supported public Finance API, or a right to automated HAMSA storage. Yahoo's general terms prohibit automated collection without express prior permission. `yfinance` is a community library, explicitly unaffiliated with Yahoo and not an official API client or license.

**Recommendation:** first obtain NSE and BSE product specifications, sample files and written terms for HAMSA's local, single-user/internal use, history retention, derived metrics and any future publication. Then validate identity/history depth and reconcile official venue observations. Consider Yahoo only for manually inspected discrepancy checks or a separately licensed/supported product. Do not make Yahoo the price, volume, corporate-action or fundamental system of record.

## Evidence labels and important limits

- **Verified capability** means an official product/page/specification describes the data or interface. It does not mean it is licensed for HAMSA's use.
- **Observed interface** means an official web page provides a view/download control; automation terms, long-term stability, complete history and data rights remain separate questions.
- **Unresolved** means available public documentation reviewed here does not establish the specific fact.
- Coverage dates are not inferred from an archive link, a UI range selector or a product marketing claim. For coverage requiring a date, the assessment records “not established” unless documentation states a defensible bound.
- The sources below are live documents and can change. Capture the applicable agreement, tariff, technical specification and version/date at procurement time.

Ticker suffix conventions such as `.NS` and `.BO` are commonly used by third-party tools, but must not be treated as an official, stable, exhaustive mapping. We did not test symbol coverage or download quotes. For HAMSA, map Yahoo tickers only as provider-specific aliases attached to an independently verified security/listing mapping, with effective dates and an unresolved state for ambiguity.

### Yahoo licensing disposition

The Yahoo US Terms of Service prohibit automated collection by robots, scrapers, data-mining or extraction tools without express prior permission. Yahoo's Australian Terms also expressly restrict creating a database/archive/feed or service that competes with or materially substitutes for Yahoo or its data providers. Terms vary by locale; the applicable HAMSA jurisdiction, user terms and source/data-provider rights must be confirmed before use. The yfinance project itself warns that it is unofficial and directs users to Yahoo's terms. Its “personal use only” notice is a project warning, not an independent grant of rights. ([US Terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html), [Australian Terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/au/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html), [yfinance README](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/blob/main/README.md))

Accordingly:

- **Personal research:** the project documentation characterizes intended use as personal research/education, but the operative Yahoo terms and source/product restrictions control. This is not a blanket right to automate, bulk store or use in a product.
- **Automated collection:** prohibited absent express prior permission under Yahoo's general terms. No HAMSA connector should be built or run on the basis of yfinance's existence.
- **Local archival:** not established as allowed for automated bulk data; depending on applicable locale and use, archival could be restricted by database/archive clauses. Yahoo's CSV export for eligible Gold subscribers is a user-directed feature, but download availability does not alone grant rights for systematic HAMSA archival.
- **Commercial/internal HAMSA:** not established or assumed. Yahoo's general API terms are API-specific; some Yahoo developer services can permit commercial use under their own terms, but this does not establish a Finance market-data license. Obtain written permission for the exact data, use, retention, users and derived outputs.
- **Redistribution/publication:** do not redistribute raw Yahoo data or expose it through a feed, report, product or public output without explicit rights. Seek express written rights, including display, derived-data and attribution terms.
- **yfinance software license:** software licensing applies to the library code, not Yahoo data. Library licensing cannot override Yahoo data terms.

The USA terms page was reviewed for the baseline and Yahoo Help is localized for the download feature. Applicable entity, jurisdiction, current terms, any India-specific addendum and the exact Yahoo Finance/data-provider contract must be confirmed by counsel or Yahoo before use. This is a product/procurement issue, not a legal conclusion.

## Historical truth, identity and data-quality implications

1. Keep issuer entity, security and venue listing distinct. Official NSE/BSE masters supply time-varying listing identifiers; retain dated source snapshots and exchange-specific codes. Routine same-issuer ISIN/symbol changes need sourced effective-dated mappings. Mergers/demergers/reorganizations remain separate entities/securities with reviewed relationships.
2. Do not use a present-day master or provider ticker to backfill historical identity. If an old symbol/ISIN mapping cannot be established from a dated official source, quarantine it.
3. Keep exchange raw OHLCV and deliverables immutable. Never average NSE/BSE prices. Venue selection and fallback must be explicit per future signal; combined traded value/volume is possible only after validation that both observations are valid, comparable and same-session. Keep venue delivery percentages separate.
4. Preserve filings and each as-reported revision. For every normalized metric retain period start/end, publication time/date, document date, actual ingestion timestamp, source filing identifier, raw-file checksum, parser and extraction version, original value/unit and normalized value/unit. Public-as-of and HAMSA-known-as-of are independent snapshots.
5. For EOD cutoff 15:30 Asia/Kolkata, time-stamped information available by cutoff may be eligible in that session; after-cutoff and date-only information is eligible next trading session. Eligibility requires the official exchange calendar including special sessions. A retrospectively discovered correction must not appear in an earlier HAMSA-known snapshot.
6. Quarantine missing/conflicting/invalid data rather than converting missing to zero or choosing a convenient venue. Model missing, stale, invalid, conflict and not-applicable separately. Reconcile duplicate filings using source IDs/checksums and preserve revisions.
7. Company accounts frequently have standalone and consolidated statements and differing scales. Define canonical units only with source-specific conversion rules; preserve original units/values. Never infer a unit from magnitude.

## Replaceable provider architecture and staged rollout

Keep provider adapters outside normalized domain logic. A provider adapter should expose capabilities and explicit product/rights metadata (venue, market, EOD-only or other frequency, fields, coverage bounds if verified, publication semantics, correction behavior, format/API, license reference and allowed retention/use). Ingestion should be contract-gated and disabled until an approved configuration identifies the licensed product and its permitted operations.

1. **Procurement/specification:** ask each exchange for EOD price and master file samples/specifications, history start by field/security state, correction/revision behavior, security-master history, corporate action event fields, calendar/special sessions, delivery definitions, file publication SLA, API/SFTP limits, local retention, internal/commercial research rights, derived metrics and redistribution terms. Obtain written terms and save the signed versions outside Git.
2. **Synthetic and schema readiness:** retain the already-built synthetic ingestion pipeline, provenance, point-in-time, identity, and lifecycle behavior. Add no provider assumptions from marketing copy.
3. **Official identity/calendar:** after licensing approval, start with dated NSE/BSE security-master snapshots and exchange calendars. Reconcile ISIN, code, series, symbol, listing lifecycle and source timestamps. Quarantine unresolved mappings.
4. **Official EOD:** ingest venue-native raw daily files, checksum/archive them, validate against counts and report totals, preserve corrections, and compare independently with the second venue where the security has a listing. Do not merge prices.
5. **Issuer filings and events:** add exchange announcements/results/actions/ownership data under approved terms, preserving filing revisions and publication times. Add company annual reports and investor presentations from official issuer/exchange sources as document provenance, with copyright/retention terms checked.
6. **Secondary checks:** only if separately authorized, use Yahoo manually or via an approved interface to inspect discrepancies. Store source label, provider symbol, retrieval/vintage, and discrepancy; never let it silently overwrite official observations.
7. **Coverage and quality audit:** measure missing dates, stale data, split/dividend jumps, duplicate rows, symbol changes, delistings and volume differences against official source files before any screening logic consumes the data.

## Unresolved questions before live ingestion

### Exchange procurement / product scope

- What precise NSE/BSE EOD product, masters, corporate-action, filing, delivery and historical packages are covered by the proposed agreement?
- What is the earliest date, instrument universe and revision depth for each field, including inactive, suspended, delisted and relisted securities?
- What are the daily publication and correction SLAs? How are late files, corrected bhavcopy, symbol/ISIN changes and historical restatements announced?
- Does the contract allow HAMSA to make a durable local raw archive and normalized local database for one internal researcher? Does internal commercial use change the fee or require a separate license?
- Are derived ratios, scores, charts and screen outputs permitted? What constitutes derived data versus redistributable market data?
- Can outputs be shown to colleagues, clients, investors, or on a hosted product? Are attribution, display delay, audit/reporting or subscriber restrictions required?
- Are the public reports and official NSE MCP historical tools covered by the same policy/contract? Can a historical-only EOD interface be licensed and technically constrained from live tools?
- Can HAMSA store issuer filing PDFs, attachments and extracted facts indefinitely? Are there exchange/provider limits on bulk download or republication?

### Yahoo-specific

- Does Yahoo offer a currently supported Finance API/product with explicit India equity daily-history entitlement, rather than consumer-site access or unofficial chart endpoints?
- Can Yahoo explicitly authorize automated retrieval, local raw archival, point-in-time snapshots, internal commercial research, derived data and internal multi-user display for HAMSA?
- Which India symbols/venues, history depth, OHLCV fields, corporate actions and financial statement bases are covered? Are historical corrections/vintages exposed?
- What do Yahoo's applicable India/jurisdiction terms and data-provider sublicenses permit, including retention after termination and redistribution?
- Can licensed Yahoo volumes be identified as NSE-specific, BSE-specific, or combined? What is the authoritative methodology and expected corrections process?

Until answered, the policy is: exchange-source research remains conditional on a suitable license; Yahoo remains a non-activated secondary candidate; no source is assumed to provide complete historical identity, financial revisions or redistribution rights.
