# HAMSA Data-Source Inventory

**As of:** 2026-11-10 (Asia/Kolkata)

**Purpose:** candidate source register for research and procurement. This is not authorization to ingest data. No providers have been activated.

## Status vocabulary

- **Candidate — licensing gate:** useful official source/product identified; use, retention or automation must be approved in writing.
- **Candidate — secondary only:** potentially useful for manual cross-checks; not a primary or licensed source assumption.
- **Specification required:** source exists, but format, coverage, historical depth or revision behavior is not sufficiently documented.
- **Deferred:** no approved implementation or activation.

## Source-selection policy

1. Official exchange/issuer observation wins for the corresponding exchange or filed fact; disagreement is recorded as a conflict, not averaged or silently overwritten.
2. NSE and BSE prices, volume and delivery remain venue-specific. Combined traded value/volume may be derived only from valid, comparable observations for the same security/session; delivery is not combined by default.
3. Yahoo is a secondary comparison candidate only. It cannot establish ISIN, venue identity, historical mappings, exchange delivery or the authoritative source of an EOD price.
4. No source is activated until its product, data fields, delivery channel, coverage, correction semantics, point-in-time behavior, archival rights, internal-use rights, derived-data rights and redistribution rights are recorded and approved.
5. Preserve source bytes immutably, along with checksum, source-specific identifier, parser/version, extraction location/time and all observation revisions. A provider correction creates a new revision; it never overwrites the prior as-reported or received value.

## Procurement questions to close

- Exact history start and completeness by security state/field, including delisted and suspended securities.
- Security-master history and ISIN/symbol change/reorganization mapping evidence.
- Corrections, filing revisions, backfills, timestamps, and point-in-time entitlements.
- EOD-only license scope and method to exclude live/intraday endpoints.
- Automated download/API/SFTP limits, local raw retention duration and database backups.
- Personal/internal research versus internal commercial use, multi-user access, derived outputs, and external redistribution/publication.
- Attribution, display-delay, audit, record-keeping, fee and termination/deletion obligations.
- Calendar/special-session coverage and delivery-data definitions.
- For Yahoo specifically: explicit permission for Finance historical-data automation, local archival, commercial/internal research and outputs; supported API/product scope for Indian equities; volume venue basis and point-in-time corrections.
