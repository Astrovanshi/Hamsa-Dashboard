### Historical coverage conclusions

- **NSE:** the strongest verifiable starting point for daily raw cash bhavcopy is March 1999, with an official historic archive description and monthly master snapshots. This is not a promise that a current subscription delivers every file or every inactive security. The daily archive catalog exists today, and modern bhavcopy formats have changed (legacy report formats were discontinued effective July 8, 2024 in favor of UDiFF), so parser versions and backward compatibility matter. ([NSE history description](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/press/Data_details_CM.pdf), [current report catalog](https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports))
- **BSE:** current daily EOD and historical price-volume/deliverable products are documented, but no dependable earliest date for full equity OHLCV or master history was found. Treat it as unknown until BSE supplies a coverage manifest/sample archive.
- **Yahoo:** history may be available for most quotes and a generic “usually not earlier than 1970” statement exists, but it is not a verifiable Indian security coverage start. Exact per-symbol gaps, suspended/no-trade dates and delisted history are unresolved.
- **CMIE:** the richest exact public historical tables found for Indian company-level research data; it has frozen twice-yearly vintages and broad historical coverage. But the standard license does not permit HAMSA database integration, so it is a research reference only unless CMIE negotiates terms that explicitly allow this use.
- **LSEG/FactSet:** broad commercial services have documented data feeds/APIs and long histories, but the reviewed public pages do not establish India-specific universe coverage, delivery metrics or an affordable HAMSA license.

### Reconciliation rules for HAMSA

1. Preserve every raw file/response and checksum. Preserve every accepted, rejected, quarantined and superseded extraction/observation revision. A later parser version is an extraction revision, not a changed source document.
2. Resolve an EOD observation by the tuple: canonical security/listing mapping effective on the session, exchange, trade date, field/definition, source product and source revision. Never collapse NSE and BSE observations into one price.
3. For a same-venue same-field conflict, prefer the latest formally identified exchange correction/republication for that product. Preserve the prior record and correction lineage. If no authoritative correction/version marker exists, quarantine the conflict for review; do not use “latest downloaded” as a proxy for authoritative revision.
4. Do not equate NSE official close, last traded price, auction price and third-party “close.” Normalize only after field definitions are proven compatible. Price differences between venues are not automatically conflicts.
5. A screening engine must declare its primary venue and fallback. If primary venue is stale, missing or invalid, use fallback only when the same-session secondary observation passes validation, label the chosen venue in the signal, and retain the alternate. Never average prices.
6. A combined traded quantity/value is allowed only by a defined calculation that sums valid same-security, same-session venue observations with compatible currencies/units and appropriate security/listing identity. Retain both inputs. Delivery quantities and percentages stay venue-specific; do not average/aggregate delivery percentages.
7. Source-provider historical adjustments are distinct derived series. Keep the exchange raw close permanently; record event terms, source/publication timestamp, factor version and formula. A factor is unavailable to point-in-time screening until its underlying action terms were public by the screening cutoff and the methodology is reviewed.
8. Restated financials are new as-reported revisions linked to filing IDs/source bytes. “Public as of” uses the reliable public timestamp and exchange calendar; “HAMSA known as of” uses actual ingestion timestamp. A later CMIE vintage or backfill cannot alter an earlier HAMSA-known snapshot.
9. Missing, no-trade, stale, invalid, conflict and not-applicable are distinct states. Never fill no row with zero. A known holiday/no session is a calendar state, not a missing EOD observation.
10. Unknown units, unmatched ISIN, ambiguous symbol reuse, incomplete action terms, unexplained cross-source discrepancies or duplicate source rows with different values go to quarantine. Do not silently repair or deduplicate on symbol/company name alone.

### Provider source-identity and accuracy notes

- Yahoo's exchange list identifies NSE/BSE suffixes and shows its price-data provider field. It lists NSE with `.NS` and BSE with `.BO`; in its consumer help, the exchange delay labels are “real-time” for NSE and 15 minutes for BSE. These are consumer quote-feed details, not a guarantee that a historical daily bar matches an official EOD file, and the page says all information is “as is,” informational, and not for trading or investing. It also states not to redistribute data. ([Yahoo exchanges/providers](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/finance/SLN2310.html))
- The same help page identifies Morningstar for international historical chart data/daily updates and ICE Data Services for the listed exchange quote source. Provider attribution does not establish a direct market-data license from either provider for HAMSA.
- Yahoo's adjusted-close help describes split-ratio factors and dividend multipliers, with pre-event values adjusted. That output is retrospective by nature: later events can change values for earlier dates. No Yahoo historical vintage or factor-publication-time archive was found, so it cannot underpin HAMSA point-in-time adjusted-price screens. ([Yahoo Adjusted Close](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN28256.html))
- `yfinance` README says the package is not affiliated, endorsed or vetted by Yahoo; says it uses Yahoo's publicly available APIs and is intended for research/education; calls Yahoo Finance API personal-use only; and notes default `auto_adjust=True`. Its software license is not a data license. Do not treat it as an official provider API or permission to automate. ([yfinance README](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/blob/main/README.md))

## Source-quality questions that remain open

### NSE/BSE

- Obtain official coverage manifests, historical sample files and security counts by year/status (active, suspended, delisted, SME) for each field.
- Confirm whether the NSE March 1999 bhavcopy/master description remains obtainable under the current licensed historical product, and exact cutoff/continuity dates for each archive type.
- Obtain BSE's oldest available daily price-volume-deliverable files and historical scrip masters, including delisted and changed-ISIN records.
- Obtain daily data delivery/publication timestamps, correction channels, corrected-file identifiers, late-file behavior and service-level guarantees.
- Establish raw field definitions: official close vs LTP, traded value currency/unit, volume adjustments, BSE/NSE delivery denominator, no-trade vs stale close, market/session handling.
- Identify exact filing-history depth and whether corrected/restated annual/quarter results are retained as separate versions with original publication timestamps.
- Obtain special-session and holiday calendar revisions, both trading and settlement calendars.
- Ask how local immutable archives, server backups, household/single-user research, derived ratios, internal use, and any future publication are treated contractually.

### Yahoo

- No per-symbol or India exchange-wide historical coverage manifest, revision/vintage API, or trustworthy earliest date was found.
- Yahoo's listed market-data provider relationship does not demonstrate that its daily bar is identical to each venue's published EOD price/volume or that Yahoo has a venue-consolidated volume methodology.
- Symbol suffixes are provider aliases; no effective-dated ISIN/company crosswalk was found.
- Current US consumer terms expressly prohibit automated collection without prior express permission; commercial, archive and derivative-output scope must be confirmed for HAMSA's jurisdiction and product. Yahoo Finance Help expressly warns against redistribution and trading/investing use.
- No written Yahoo permission, data use agreement or official public Indian-equity API documentation is in the research record.

### Third-party alternatives

- CMIE v1 agreement and all its interfaces are not interchangeable. Request a product-specific enterprise license before considering any integration; standard ProwessDX's noncommercial/no-database/no-automation conditions are a hard blocker.
- For LSEG or FactSet, obtain an India security universe/identifier mapping file, delisted-security treatment, per-field starting date, venue/source field, corporate-action factor history, corrections, schedule and retention rights in a trial/contract. Do not accept the existence of a global coverage claim as India coverage evidence.
