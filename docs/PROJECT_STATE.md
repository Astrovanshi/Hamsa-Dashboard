# HAMSA Project State

## Current architecture

HAMSA is a local, EOD-only Investment Research and Portfolio Intelligence System. Its original opportunity-discovery purpose remains; the five discovery workbenches are Compounders, Earnings Inflection, Capex Conversion, Cash Quality, and Extreme Sell-Side Liquidity. No automatic buy/sell recommendations are permitted. Python 3.11, DuckDB 2.0.0, SQLite, and Streamlit are the approved stack; no new dependencies are authorized without approval.

DuckDB holds sourced analytical history; SQLite holds human review history. Raw files are immutable and checksum-addressed. Observations are revisioned and append-only through repository interfaces. Issuer, security, exchange listing, company, account, position, and transaction identities remain separate. NSE/BSE listings can share one ISIN-based security while retaining distinct venue observations.

The investment universe is exclusively officially classified NSE/BSE mainboard ordinary equity. SME/Emerge and non-equity records may be reconciled separately but never enter the universe. Eligibility derives from reviewed identity, approved effective listing status, instrument class, platform class, and point-in-time source availability. Snapshot dates do not manufacture effective dates. `public_as_of` and `hamsa_known_as_of` remain independent.

Official exchange-file ingestion remains the primary daily source path. TejHQ Parquet is now the approved primary source for historical EOD backfill, while remaining distinct from official observations and subject to HAMSA identity, classification, provenance, revision, calendar and point-in-time controls. The bounded local-file TejHQ pilot supports raw NSE/BSE OHLCV/turnover only; adjusted prices, corporate actions and delivery are excluded. Full historical backfill remains unapproved.

## Blockers and limitations

- TejHQ data-use terms do not establish exchange licensing; this milestone is limited to the previously authorized local technical pilot and makes no broader licensing claim.
- The retained NSE 2026 and 2010 samples are tied to exact immutable file commits and matching checksums. The retained BSE 2026 sample does not match the current published BSE file hash/revision, so its exact source commit is unresolved and it cannot support provenance-complete import.
- Official project rules leave NSE SBI ordinary-equity classification unresolved, and BSE current mainboard/ordinary classification is not verified. Actual samples cannot be admitted as eligible observations until approved evidence is ingested and reviewed.
- TejHQ rows may have missing ISINs; such rows remain unresolved. BSE Parquet does not contain the BSE scrip code; approved listing mapping is required.
- Only one-session SBI local-byte parity was verified for each venue. NSE sample provenance is now verified; BSE sample provenance is not. No broad parity or full-universe coverage claim is allowed.

No new architectural milestone is proposed. Resume ordinary local operation after the existing Streamlit dependency is available and source owners provide the official files and classification evidence listed above. No full backfill, broker integration, cloud or live/intraday feed is in this MVP. No automatic buy/sell recommendations are produced; portfolio intelligence remains future work.
